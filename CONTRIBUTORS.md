## Upstream authors

libbink2 is derived from work by the following people. They wrote the code
it was ported from and are not otherwise affiliated with this project.

- Konstantin Shishkov and Paul B Mahol: the unmerged FFmpeg Bink 2 video
  decoder (`bink2.c`, `bink2f.c`, `bink2g.c`).
- Peter Ross and Daniel Verkamp: FFmpeg's `binkaudio.c`.
- Sean Barrett and the stb contributors: `stb_image.h`, used by the encoder's
  image loader.

Exact files, copyright years, and licenses are in
[docs/bink2.md](docs/bink2.md#attribution).
